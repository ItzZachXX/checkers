How to play
  • Pieces move one square diagonally forward onto dark squares.
  • Jump over an opponent's piece to capture it. If you can capture, you must.
  • Keep jumping in the same turn while more captures are available.
  • Reach the far row to become a king. Kings move forward and backward.
  • You win when your opponent has no pieces or no legal moves.
